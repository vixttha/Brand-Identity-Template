## Findings

| Area | Finding |
| --- | --- |
| Brief and concept selection | The brief selected Facet before documenting competing categories, art styles, ranked concepts or the rationale for rejecting alternatives. The portable template made all of those decisions optional. |
| Logo | The dark wedge and mint plane read as a directional arrow or folded paper at a glance. The concept uses clean geometry but has little visual detail tied specifically to shaping and repairing 3D assets. |
| Wordmark | The four uppercase letters separate at 120 px and 160 px in the recorded raster check. Wide tracking and generic squared forms dominate. The angular A and R offer a weak visual link to the symbol. |
| Color and applications | The palette has checked contrast pairs. The PNGs are light-surface drafts. Circle crop, minimum icon size, dark use, one-color reproduction, and a combined lockup remain incomplete. |
| Voice and naming | The existing product name, agent name and slogan are documented. The original workflow could proceed from that record without a fresh owner check on the exact wording to be drawn. |
| PNG checks and Graphify | The checks covered repository structure, PNG metadata, digests and Graphify read-back. The owner's rejection is the outcome of creative review. |
| Portability | The migrated kit passes adoption into a separate Git repository. The verified v0.0.1 PNG inventory has no fields for a scored shortlist or recorded name confirmation. |

## Proposed direction before artwork

Initial category: pictorial mark. Lead art style: relief-cut printmaking. The concept should tell a story about an unfinished form becoming deliberate while keeping a clear outline in one color.

| Screened category | Fit |
| --- | --- |
| Pictorial | Strong. A sculptural form can make the action of shaping visible. |
| Lettermark | Good. Custom lettering can carry the identity when the product name is the focus. |
| Emblem | Good for a documentation badge. A border can compete with the subject at avatar scale. |
| Abstract mark | Weak for this brief. The Facet draft already shows how quickly generic geometry becomes ambiguous. |
| Mascot | Weak for this brief. A character would pull attention toward the name association and away from the 3D workflow. |

Scores are creative judgments before artwork. Criteria in order are brand fit, artistic character, distinctiveness, and versatility, each out of five.

| Concept | Category and style | Drawing idea and trade-off | Scores | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Shape in Relief** | Pictorial · Relief-cut printmaking | One sculptural form with a rough edge resolving into a clean cut. Carved negative space shows the transformation. The small mark has room for only the main cut. | 5 / 5 / 4 / 4 | **92** |
| Cut Letter | Lettermark · Custom engraved lettering | A carved B with two counters that suggest a cut through solid material. It can hold up as an icon, though it says less about mesh work on its own. | 4 / 4 / 5 / 5 | **88** |
| Meshmaker's Seal | Emblem · Workshop print stamp | An unbordered mesh-repair motif tested in a circular stamp for docs. The broader badge carries detail, while the standalone avatar will need a simpler crop. | 4 / 5 / 4 / 3 | **82** |

**Selected for the next drawing session: Shape in Relief.** It gives BART a visible action, the moment a rough form becomes intentional, and leaves room for a matching carved wordmark. The proposed name strings for this artwork are **Blender-Agent Refinement Toolkit** and **BART**. The existing naming decision supplies context. The drawing session still needs an explicit owner response.
