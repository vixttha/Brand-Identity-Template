# Wordmark direction

Use the exact working text **Branchwell** in title case. Set it in a compact humanist sans with clear differentiation between lowercase r, n and w. Keep normal horizontal proportions and tighten spacing optically around the uppercase B.

Place the mark on the leading edge, aligned to the wordmark's optical center. A navigation lockup uses one glyph width of spacing. Keep a text-only variation for narrow surfaces. Review legibility on paper and graphite before registering artwork.
