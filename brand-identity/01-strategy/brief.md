# Strategy and creative brief

**Category.** Pre-alpha Python tooling for inspecting, creating, converting, and repairing 3D assets locally. The CLI and optional MCP adapter provide two entry points. Mesh operations use headless Blender where required.

**Audience.** Developers and technical artists testing a repeatable way to inspect or refine 3D assets. Their first questions are what BART does, what formats and operations it supports, and which steps require Blender.

**First surface.** GitHub and documentation. A reader should see the product name and purpose quickly and recognize the same identity in the project avatar.

**Visual direction proposal.** Facet. A dense outline with one diagonal gap gives the logo its identity. Ink provides weight. A small mint face brings focus to the cut. The wordmark has square shoulders and repeats the diagonal in its letterforms.

**Visual boundaries.** Keep the design product-specific. Exclude mascots and recognizable entertainment, transit, and third-party software marks. Favor mesh geometry visible in the tool's actual examples.

**Success criteria.** Spell BART exactly. Recognize the split at the intended display size. Keep the surrounding product line legible in light and dark themes. Keep product and agent names identifiable independently.
