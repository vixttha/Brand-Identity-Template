# BART logo concepts and design review

## Identity and decision state

The locked product name is **Blender-Agent Refinement Toolkit**. Its agent is **BART**. The current public brand line is **“Give shape to an idea.”** The slogan alternatives and logo concepts below are written proposals. Artwork selection, exact wordmark layout approval, and public use remain pending. [Naming decision](https://github.com/vixttha/Blender-Agent-Refinement-Toolkit/issues/62)

The creative direction builds on a specific product behavior. The toolkit can inspect an asset, perform a bounded operation, and write a separate result while preserving the input. The proposed marks use the relationship between the original and the result to express the maker's control. [Product capability evidence](https://github.com/vixttha/Blender-Agent-Refinement-Toolkit/blob/3e7f9e02e61739825b56bcafecebbd0221cc9657/README.md)

## Historical draft and lessons

- **Facet was rejected in creative review.** The mint wedge and split diagonal suggest an arrow or folded paper. The dark wordmark is legible in the recorded 120 px and 160 px raster checks, and its wide tracking and squared letters give it a generic appearance. The angled A and R had a weak relationship to the symbol.
- The two transparent PNGs, Ink/Mist/Mint palette, and Geist type proposal document that draft. Placement across themes, circular crop, minimum icon size, one-color rendering, and the combined layout remained open.
- The first workflow moved from brief to one design without a ranked comparison of categories and styles. It could also proceed without asking for an explicit decision on the exact name in the artwork. The revised method calls for category and style comparison, separately ranked or reviewed lists for name, logo, slogan and wordmark, and three meanings supported by a design detail and project evidence.
- The first creative audit considered Shape in Relief, Cut Letter, and Meshmaker’s Seal. Shape in Relief is the relief-print predecessor of The Held Cut. The scores in that audit were written concept judgments. The expanded ranking below records the later review.
- The original asset and Graphify checks verified the committed files and source map. Creative acceptance was a separate review. The later v0.0.2 adoption trial is tracked in its own draft PR.

## Ranked logo concepts

This is a qualitative creative ranking based on relation to the product, artistic character, and expected readability of the main idea at icon size.

| Rank | Concept and style | Drawing | Three meanings tied to the form |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **The Held Cut**, hand-carved relief | Two profiles share an unbroken baseline. One keeps its rough edge and the other shows a clean chisel cut. | **Preservation** in the untouched profile. **Refinement** in the cut. **Judgment** in the common baseline, which lets the maker compare the starting point and result. |
| 2 | **The Open Joint**, cut paper | Two irregular pieces meet at one bridge, with a deliberate opening beside the join. | **Repair** in the bridge, echoing a vertex weld. **Inspection** in the opening that exposes the join. **Restraint** in the outer ends that retain their original character. |
| 3 | **The Cut Letter**, engraved lettermark | A custom B has one rough counter, one precise counter and a continuous spine. | **Identity** in the agent's initial. **Iteration** in two treatments of the same letter. **Continuity** in the spine holding both states together. |
| 4 | **The Open Frame**, architectural ink | Three measured corner brackets enclose an irregular solid. The fourth corner stays open. | **Measurement** in the brackets, recalling reported bounds. **Attention** in the solid's silhouette. **Creative freedom** in the corner that leaves room to act. |
| 5 | **Meshmaker’s Seal**, printmaker’s stamp | An uneven circular impression surrounds three vertices and one engraved face. | **Structure** in the vertices defining a face. **Craft** in the carved impression. **Traceability** in the stamp's association with recording work. |

## Slogan options

| Slogan proposal | Three meanings |
| --- | --- |
| **Keep the source. Shape what comes next.** | **Keep the source** refers to preserving the input. **Shape** gives the maker the creative decision. **What comes next** treats the separate result as a version to compare, keep or refine. |
| **See the shape. Choose the change.** | **See** anchors the process in inspection. **Choose** puts the operation in the maker's hands. **The change** describes a specific action whose result can be assessed. |

**Recommended pairing.** The Held Cut with “Keep the source. Shape what comes next.” The original outline makes preservation visible and the carved edge shows the operation. The Open Joint is the strongest alternate when mesh repair is the primary message.

## Artwork review targets

1. Compare The Held Cut and The Open Joint as black marks at **32 px**, with the same background and no label. Simplify the cut or join if its shape disappears.
2. Verify the finalized symbol at **16 px and 32 px** for browser tabs and at **48 px** in a circular avatar crop. Compare the silhouette with adjacent 3D tools.
3. Assess the three visible meaning details, exact BART lettering, one-color reversal, light/dark placement, and the combined symbol/wordmark layout. Record minimum size, clear space, gap and approved backgrounds.
4. If a concept is approved, prepare the master, SVG, transparent PNGs, and favicon exports as needed. Verify first-contact copy uses the full product name. A standalone home-link logo needs an accessible name such as “BART home.”
5. Review similar names and marks for the intended public use. Record owner decisions and preserve the existing draft PNG hashes and generation notes.
