## Generation provenance

### Facet logo

Use case: logo-brand. Create a standalone production-minded icon proposal for BART, an open-source toolkit for inspecting and refining 3D meshes. One strong unmistakable emblem, rendered as a centered isolated flat vector-like silhouette on true transparent alpha, large on square canvas with about 12% optically centered safety padding. The emblem is a forward-leaning triangular mesh facet: overall shape is an asymmetrical triangular wedge pointing to the upper right, with a broad deep ink navy foundation and one smaller vivid mint-green facet inserted along the upper-right diagonal, separated by a thick diagonal slit of real transparent negative space. Think careful architectural tool brand, robust at favicon scale, reduce the silhouette to two large heavy planar shapes, make the diagonal break distinctive and short. Pixel clean edges, simple, one solid fill per facet. Colors approximately #17252D and #38E6A8. Avoid text entirely, frames, outlines, tiny pieces, gloss, gradient, shadow, generic hexagons, Blender icon or other recognizable brand marks. No mockup, no checkerboard painted into the image.

### Wordmark

Use case: logo-brand. Render a single refined typographic mark that reads exactly BART, four uppercase Latin letters, on actual transparent background. Style: geometric editorial grotesk, bold but not extra-black, normal to slightly wide proportions, clean flat dark ink #17252D, straight cuts at letter terminals, wide triangular A counter, diagonal R leg. The defining visual treatment is very deliberate WIDE tracking like a refined technical manufacturer: four clearly separated glyphs, with nearly a full letter-stem thickness of clear horizontal air between adjacent glyph bounding boxes (roughly 40 native pixels between the rightmost point of the A foot and the vertical left of R). NO tight kerning or touching, intentional clear gaps that read even as a 120 px-wide word. Preserve generous margins around the whole design. No logo symbol, no extra words or punctuation. No warping, gradients, bevels, shadows, grain, textures, or checkerboard. Transparent alpha around glyphs and within counters. Wide horizontal composition.

The earlier wordmark explorations had tight clearances between letterforms. The selected version uses wider spacing. The wordmark and logo are review drafts. Reversed, one-color, and combined production exports remain open. The MIT notice covers the reusable template documents and validation code.
